Willowbrook, Illinois Location

The Acupuncture Health Center is located in Willowbrook, a western suburb of Chicago. Our office can be found directly south of the Hinsdale Nursery, located in a business park one block west of Madison Street on 73rd Street.

Acupuncture Health Center

We are conveniently located within a few minutes’ drive of the cities of Burr Ridge, Hinsdale, Darien, Downers Grove and Western Springs.
